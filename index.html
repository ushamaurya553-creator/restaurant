Our Story

Where Flavour
Meets Finesse

White Charcoal was born from a singular obsession — to craft a dining experience that transcends the ordinary. Nestled in the heart of Andheri West, our restaurant blends the warmth of Indian hospitality with the sophistication of global fine dining.

Every plate that leaves our kitchen carries the signature of our world-class chefs — masters of flavour who source only the finest ingredients, celebrating seasonality and provenance with every dish.

From our intimate dining room lit by amber candlelight to the sultry notes of live jazz drifting through the air, White Charcoal invites you to slow down, savour, and celebrate.
